Short answers.

Is this a real launch?

Yes. The transaction goes to the Pons V2 launch factory (0x7eD598Bc…EC7e, verified on Blockscout). The coin shows on ponsfamily.com like any other launch, on a bonding curve that graduates to Uniswap v4 at the same threshold.

Does REVIVE hold my coin, my ETH or my keys?

No. There is no REVIVE contract. Your wallet signs one call to the Pons factory and pays Pons' launch fee. We can't pause, take, or redirect anything.

Can I revive a coin that isn't mine?

You're launching a new token with the same name, ticker and logo. Anyone can, and everyone can see the old CA and the new one. Be upfront in your post. Devs can revive their own dead coins in one click, with fee-share on this time.

Where does the logo come from?

On a revive, the old token's on-chain ipfs:// logo is reused — no upload. For a new coin, paste an ipfs:// or https image URL.

What does the creator tax do?

Pons takes it on every trade and pays it to the fee recipient you set. From your Pons profile you can flip it to holder fee-sharing. Max 10%.

Why is it free?

Because $REVIVE pays for it. The creator tax on $REVIVE trades goes to $REVIVE holders. The people who hold the coin fund the graveyard.

What are the gravestones?

Real Pons launches that never graduated. Click one and its CA drops into the console. The rest of the field is filled with stones for the ones we haven't named yet — there are a lot.
